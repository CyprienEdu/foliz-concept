Our story

From a small village in the Gers

Foliz was created by Gwendal and Charline in Cologne, in the Gers, where we still brew every bottle by hand. Fermented tea, real fruit, plants and spices: a natural alternative to industrial sodas. And because we love Toulouse, you'll find us riding the Foliz Bike through its streets.
